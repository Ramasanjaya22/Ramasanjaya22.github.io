The student deck: eight concept projects, 2021–2024

9 Nov 2026 · 2 min read

Before BYRIS there were eight concept projects, done between 2021 and 2024 while I was a student at Telkom University — AR shopping, AI reading tools, mixed-reality training, an accessibility app. This is the deck I pitched them with, exported slide by slide. Design and concept work: it shows how I think about products, not production systems. The production systems live in the work section.

These slides are the honest artifact: no rebuild, no restyle. PowerPoint exported to PNG, compressed to WebP with FFmpeg, and published as they were pitched — including the email and phone number on every footer, which I would not put on a slide today.
