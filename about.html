What I do

I started as a frontend developer who cared too much about how things move. Fifteen months at PT Eltran taught me the other half: the database, the contracts, and the part where a feature has to survive real users.

Since early 2026 I have worked independently, building products rather than screens. BYRIS is the clearest example: a directory of Indonesian suppliers that became an evidence-graded intelligence platform, because a listing you cannot verify is not data, it is a rumour.

I write the schema, the matching and the interface. That usually means Python for collection, PostgreSQL for the source of truth, Go for the matching pass, and SvelteKit for the desk.
